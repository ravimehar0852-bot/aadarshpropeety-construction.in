Rail Vihar — A Luxury Residential Township

Plots & Villas on Canal Road, Opp. Royal Marriage Garden, Borkhera, Kota. Open to all buyers — railway employment not required.

You Can Get Your Home In A Good Society

Thoughtfully Planned. Built For Families.

Rail Vihar is a KDA-approved township developed by the Indian Railway Welfare Organization and powered by Aadarsh Group. Anyone can buy a plot or villa here — being a railway employee is not required.

The colony is laid out with wide cemented roads, underground water supply, round-the-clock electricity and a full range of community amenities — designed as a complete, self-sufficient residential township.

  • Government / corporation water pipeline in colony
  • 40 ft wide main link road, 30 ft CC internal roads
  • Underground water tank with power pump — 24x7 supply
  • 5 transformers — uninterrupted 24x7 electricity
  • Solar & electric street lighting throughout
  • Boundary wall around the entire colony
  • Land reserved for a hospital facility
  • Guarded main entrance with CCTV surveillance
